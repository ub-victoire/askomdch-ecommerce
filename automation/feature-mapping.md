## Readiness guidance

- All candidates require review of the original Jira description and linked Story acceptance criteria before implementation.
- AEC-101 and AEC-115 have no Story link and no description; do not assign them to a confirmed feature until traceability and test intent are clarified.
- AEC-115 additionally conflicts with the AEC-3 Epic description, which lists promotional coupon application/removal as out of scope.
- AEC-124, AEC-125, and AEC-131 need a controlled mailbox/reset-link mechanism if delivery and link use are to be automated.
- AEC-95, AEC-96, and AEC-99 need reliable browser/session lifecycle handling; AEC-99 also needs its scenario clarified.
- AEC-144 and AEC-145 require an approved non-production order/payment setup. AEC-103 requires a confirmed account/address fixture.
- No Test is declared permanently unsuitable based only on this CSV. Automation suitability must be confirmed after manual-case clarification and environment review.
- Do not store credentials, passwords, customer data, or production secrets in this repository.
