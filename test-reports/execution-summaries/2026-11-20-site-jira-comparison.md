## Differences and questions to investigate

1. **Search wording:** AEC-15 specifies a search field labelled “Search product:” and a heading `Search results for “{query}”`. The inspected store search field's accessible label was “Search for:”; the search-page **H1** was `Search results: “shirt”` for `shirt`. A breadcrumb used “Search results for …”. Clarify whether the criterion targets the H1 or breadcrumb and whether the field label should change. These observations alone are not a complete AEC-18 result.
2. **Price range display:** On the Accessories category, the three displayed current prices were $10, $25, and $75, while the initial slider showed $10–$80. For the direct $10–$25 request, the slider displayed $10–$30 even though the URL retained `max_price=25`. AEC-11 describes category inventory bounds and retaining submitted slider positions. Verify slider increments and an actual UI submission before deciding whether this is a defect or intended rounding.
3. **Billing phone:** A previously supplied checkout screenshot showed “Phone (optional)”, whereas [AEC-23](../../requirements/epics-overview.md#aec-23) requires a non-empty phone. This is user-provided visual evidence, not a fresh populated-checkout execution; required-field behavior remains unverified.
4. **Registration email wording:** The separate [account report](2026-11-20-read-only-account.md) records a browser-native validation wording difference from AEC-5. Browser and locale may affect the wording.
5. **Unresolved Jira definitions:** [AEC-81](../../test-cases/browsing-products/AEC-81.md) has a cart-oriented title but an exported AEC-16 link. [AEC-101](../../test-cases/untraced/AEC-101.md) and [AEC-115](../../test-cases/untraced/AEC-115.md) lack exported Story links and descriptions; AEC-115 also raises the existing coupon-scope question. Site observations do not establish missing Jira relationships, steps, or an invalid-coupon message. See [traceability gaps](../../requirements/requirements-traceability.md#traceability-gaps-and-concerns).
